## Base

The game began as Unity Learn's Junior Programmer pathway, Unit 3 "Sound and Effects":
Prototype 3 (a runner that jumps over obstacles on a scrolling background, with particles,
sound effects and music) and Challenge 3 "Balloons, Bombs, & Booleans" (a balloon that floats
while a key is held, collects money and is blown up by bombs). The ideas of jumping obstacles
on a looping backdrop and of a floating balloon among money and bombs come from that course.

## Removed content

`Assets/Course Library` (the course's characters, obstacles, textures, materials, particle
prefabs, animations, music and sound effects), `Assets/Challenge 3` (the challenge's scene,
prefabs, sounds and outcome video), the runner scene with its obstacle prefab and material,
and `game-background-png-6-Transparent-Images.png` were removed. The course library's own
`_LICENSE.txt` says its Synty Studios models are "not for commercial use" and that its music
and sound effects by cabled_mess and Cron Audio were provided for the course; none of it may
be redistributed in a public repository. The background PNG was downloaded from the web with
no known author or licence. The 2021 editor logs and per-user settings were untracked.
History was not rewritten.
